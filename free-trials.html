Quick answers

Are Amazon free trials really free?

Yes — during the trial period you are not charged. When the trial ends, the membership renews automatically at the regular price unless you cancel first, so set a reminder for a few days before the trial ends and decide with fresh eyes.

Who qualifies for Prime for Young Adults?

Anyone aged 18–24 qualifies on age alone — no school enrollment needed — and enrolled college students qualify too. It includes a 6-month $0 trial, then costs $7.49/mo or $69/yr.

What is Prime Access?

Prime Access is full Prime at half price — $6.99/mo after a 30-day free trial — for people who qualify through programs like SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, WIC, TANF or LIHEAP, or with income verification.

Can I cancel a trial before it charges me?

Yes. Every trial on this page can be cancelled from your Amazon account before the trial period ends, and cancelling stops the paid membership from starting.
