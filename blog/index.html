Guides that do the homework first

Every guide on this blog is built the same way: pick a budget people actually shop with, then fill it only with recognizable brands whose prices and review histories were checked before publishing. That is why the lists are ten products long instead of a hundred — most of what gets marketed as a "deal" does not survive a price-history check, and we would rather leave it out than pad a guide with it. New guides are added as seasons and budgets demand them, and every guide explains how its picks were chosen, with honest trade-offs and answers to the questions buyers ask most.
