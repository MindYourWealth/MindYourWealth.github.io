Black Friday 2026 Watchlist: The 10 Products Worth Waiting For

October 21, 2026

Black Friday 2026 lands on Friday, November 27 — with Cyber Monday right behind it on November 30 — and it is the best week of the year to buy the right things. The catch is the word "right." Plenty of Black Friday "deals" are ordinary prices wearing a costume. This page is the opposite of that: a short watchlist of ten products with a long, boring, reliable history of taking their deepest discounts of the year during Black Friday week.

Think of this as a plan, not a sale. Bookmark the products you actually want, learn what a good price looks like for each one, and then — when the prices start moving in late November — you will be checking out while everyone else is still comparison-shopping. We will refresh this page as Black Week deals go live, so the picks below are the ones to keep an eye on between now and November 27.

How this watchlist works

  • Proven sale patterns, not predictions. Every pick is here because it (or its direct predecessor) reliably goes on deep sale for Black Friday — Amazon's own devices, the two most-gifted kitchen appliances in America, and the stocking stuffers that fly every December.
  • Recognizable brands only. Echo, Instant Pot, Ninja, JBL, Anker, LEGO, Laneige. Black Friday is when brand-name gear finally costs what the no-name stuff costs the rest of the year.
  • No printed prices — on purpose. Prices move too much between now and November 27 for a number printed in October to mean anything. Each pick tells you what a good price looks like instead, and the button takes you to the live price.
  • Mostly giftable, mostly modest. Almost everything here is the kind of thing people buy in late November: gifts, home upgrades, and the small stuff that fills stockings.

What a good Black Friday price looks like

A real Black Friday deal has one tell: it beats the price the product sells for the rest of the year — not the inflated "list price" printed next to it, which exists mainly to make discounts look bigger. Amazon's own devices (Echo, Fire TV) historically take their steepest cuts of the entire year during Black Friday week, often beating even Prime Day. Big kitchen brands like Instant Pot and Ninja typically match or beat their Prime Day lows. And the small stuff — pillows, lip masks, hand warmers — tends to get bundled, couponed, or marked down as the gift-buying wave peaks. If a "doorbuster" price is one you have already seen in October, it is not a doorbuster. Wait.

What to skip until November 27

Three things are almost never worth buying at full price in October: Amazon hardware (the discounts are coming, and they are the deepest of the year), small kitchen appliances you are buying as gifts (Instant Pot and Ninja plan their year around this week), and seasonal consumables like hand warmers, which get cheaper as winter demand peaks and retailers compete for the stocking-stuffer basket. Everything below is on this list precisely because patience pays for it.

1. Echo Dot (5th Gen) — the Black Friday device deal, every single year

Amazon's best-selling smart speaker is the most predictable deal in all of Black Friday: Amazon discounts its own hardware harder than anything else it sells, and the Dot is the device it pushes hardest. The 5th-gen model brings bigger, clearer sound than earlier Dots, a temperature sensor for smart-home routines, and tap-to-snooze. Buy it for the bedroom, the kitchen, or the gift list — at Black Friday pricing it is the cheapest way into Alexa that exists.

  • Pros: the most reliably discounted device of the season; noticeably better sound than older Dots; doubles as a smart-home hub starter.
  • Cons: no clock display on this version (that is the separate Dot with Clock variant); needs Wi-Fi and an outlet, obviously.

Best for: first smart speaker, bedroom/kitchen Alexa, and the easiest under-the-tree tech gift.

A good price looks like a steep cut from its everyday street price — if it is barely discounted in November, you are looking at the wrong week.

Best for: older TVs, second rooms, dorms, and anyone whose built-in TV apps have gone slow.

Quick answers

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 is Friday, November 27, with Cyber Monday on Monday, November 30. In practice, Amazon's Black Friday pricing usually starts rolling out across Black Week in the days before the 27th — which is exactly why this watchlist exists: know your targets before the prices start moving.

Should I buy now or wait for Black Friday?

For anything on this list, wait. These are products with a long track record of taking their deepest discounts of the year during Black Friday week. If you need something today, buy the need — but for gifts and upgrades, the same item is very likely to cost less in late November.

How do I know a Black Friday price is actually good?

Compare the sale price against the price the item actually sells for the rest of the year — not the inflated list price printed next to it. A real Black Friday deal beats the product's usual street price by a clear margin; a fake one just beats a list price nobody ever paid.

What if a watchlist item sells out on Black Friday?

Check again on Cyber Monday (November 30) — Amazon regularly restocks or re-runs popular device and kitchen deals then. And add the item to your cart or a wishlist now: a cart add is the fastest way to check out the moment the price drops.
